Sahaj Talwar

Creator · Self-Improvement and Life Coaching
Personal Brand · Content Strategy · Organic Lead Generation  ·  Oct 2024 to Apr 2026
Who they are
  • Self-improvement and life coaching creator with real on-camera presence
  • Wanted a personal brand in the mould of Hamza Ahmed, the self-improvement YouTuber
  • No audience and no engine turning his frameworks into reach
What they wanted
  • Build a personal brand and position him as an authority in the niche
  • Turn that following into organic leads for his life coaching business
  • Leave a base he could later monetise through coaching or a digital product
The creator

What we did.

  • Positioned him as an expert in self-improvement rather than a general motivation account
  • Built content pillars off that positioning so every reel reinforced one authority claim
  • Every reel shipped with three hook variations, tested on Instagram Trial Reels before anything went public
  • Winners posted, losers banked, so the account only ever showed the audience a proven hook
  • Built an organic lead funnel on top of the reach once the audience existed
